# Code of Conduct

SmartFarmOS is committed to a respectful, safe, and constructive community.
This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and project-related events.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Keep safety claims honest, especially around farms, devices, and automation.
- Accept correction and focus on the impact of behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private farm data, participant data, or vulnerability
  details in public channels.
- Misrepresenting simulator behavior as physical-device or safety evidence.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public-thread problem, flag the content and disengage. For a
confidential report, contact the repository maintainers through an available
private GitHub channel. If no private channel is available, open an issue titled
`Conduct contact requested` without incident details so a maintainer can arrange
a private route.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, without promising confidentiality
that the available platform cannot provide.
